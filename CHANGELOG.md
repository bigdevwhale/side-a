# Changelog

## [1.0.1]

- **Songs by button is on for new installs:** previous and next sit in the listening room from the start.
- **Clearer hints in the room:** how to change songs, and that tapping the tonearm raises and lowers the needle.
- **Spotify setup explains itself:** when Spotify doesn't recognise the app, settings say which package and SHA‑1 to add in Spotify's dashboard.

## [1.0.0]

The first release.

- **Records or cassettes:** pick a look at the start (and change it in settings): a walnut hi‑fi with a turntable, or a silver tape deck. Each comes in its own light or dark theme, or follows the phone's.
- **The record shop:** one album a week, on the delivery day you pick; up to six put aside on the counter; the first three free.
- **Three ways to bring an album home:** from what your streaming player is playing, from a folder of your own files, or by name.
- **The shelf:** twenty‑four places, spines on walnut shelves or cassettes in a rack. When it's full, one goes to the second‑hand shop with a farewell line, and can be bought back.
- **The listening room:** silence before the needle, side A and then turn it over, a tonearm to drag (and scratch with), piano keys and real‑time winding for cassettes, VU or LED meters. Picking up the phone lifts the needle.
- **Wear:** sleeves, spines, vinyl and labels age with listening; worn records crackle more, stretched tapes wobble more.
- **Listening along** with Spotify, YouTube Music, Yandex Music and others through notification access: the album is recognised, its songs learnt, skips scratch the record. *Play the album* starts it from the room where the player allows it.
- **Real recordings (optional):** CC0 vinyl crackle and tape hiss, under folder records and over your streaming app.
- **Spotify without opening it (optional):** with your own Spotify Client ID (Settings → Spotify shows how), *Play the album* starts it in Spotify quietly, from the first song, and *Browse your Spotify* in the shop brings albums from your recent plays, library and picks to the counter.
- **Find any album in Spotify:** search its whole catalogue from the shop, or *Share → Side A* from Spotify; the album comes to the counter with its cover, year and songs. This is the one thing that uses the internet.
- **Songs by button (optional):** previous and next in the room; the needle lifts and drops by itself, a cassette skips the winding, and a skip from the room never scratches.
- **The inner sleeve:** a line written after listening, kept with the record.
- English and Russian.
